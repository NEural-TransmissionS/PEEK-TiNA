### E1 — falsification audit on a really-pruned YOLO26n

10 arms (magnitude/random × 10–50% head-channel budget), 64 calib / 64 test
COCO128 images, τ = logit(0.25), 555 audited (image, class, level) triples/arm.

- **`violations @ ε_cert` = 0 on every arm** (5550 certified-or-not triples). The
  sound certificate's falsifiable prediction holds.
- Certified coverage @ ε_cert: **27.9%** at 10% magnitude budget → 4.0% → 0.4% →
  ~0 by 40%. Honest-but-weak, exactly as the plan's §3.2 warns; the empirical
  bound covers 55%→20% but is **not sound** (see below).
- **`violations @ ε_emp` = 1** on 3 arms — each is an out-of-distribution case
  where the calibration-set ε under-covered the test perturbation
  (`epsilon_bounded_the_gap: false`). Not a certificate bug; it is the reason
  ε_emp is a diagnostic only (plan §3.2b).
- Looseness ε_cert / ε_emp ≈ **2–5×**. Pruning changes output topology on
  40→127 of 555 triples as the budget grows — the metric is sensitive.

### E2 (P1.5) — synthetic validation, exact homology

240 constructed scenes × 5 smooth perturbations per δ. **0 constructed-vs-computed
Betti mismatches. 0 audit violations at every δ** (0.5→6.0). Coverage falls
smoothly 100% → 2.9% as δ grows past the scenes' margins. Targeted check: a merge
perturbation changed Betti on 90 of 165 multi-component scenes and was certified
as topology-preserving on **0**.
